Interpretation and limits

The 2022 baseline is the US Senate race, not the 2022 HD32 House race. The current 2026 district covers all of Granville and six current Vance precincts. The absentee file is a snapshot through October 23, not a live feed. Registered party does not identify votes for a candidate. This dashboard does not infer a winner from turnout or registration; the external model panel reports previously discussed third-party figures separately.
